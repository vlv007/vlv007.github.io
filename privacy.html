Don't Brake: Police Chase

Privacy Policy · Effective 11 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Don't Brake: Police Chase (the "App", Android package com.luanto.dontbrake), made by Luan To ("we"), uses and why.

Short version

  • The App has no accounts, no sign-in and no in-app purchases.
  • We do not collect, store or sell your personal information on any server of ours.
  • The App shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device data to show and measure ads, as described below.

Information stored on your device only

The App saves your best distance, your sound and vibration settings, and whether you have seen the tutorial. This stays on your device, is never sent to us, and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows banner and interstitial ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID),
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language),
  • interactions with ads (views and taps) and diagnostic data such as crash information.

How Google uses this information is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): when you first open the App, a consent form from Google lets you choose whether ads may be personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad consent inside the App.
  • Personalised ads: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Uninstall: uninstalling the App stops all collection by the App and deletes the data stored on your device.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the App are limited to content suitable for a general audience with parental guidance.

Security and retention

We keep no personal information on our own servers. Information processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies linked above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the App's store listing.
